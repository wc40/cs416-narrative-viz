Two caveats on the left-hand chart. The two series share a log axis but not a unit, so their vertical separation is meaningless — only the slopes are comparable. And a country whose lines move together over eleven years has not thereby demonstrated causation; both series respond to the business cycle, and 2020 in particular is distorted by the pandemic's demand shock.

Data: Our World in Data — Energy and World Bank WDI. Our World in Data publishes a full source breakdown for 56 countries; Hong Kong is excluded here because the World Bank does not report an adjusted net national income series for it, leaving 55. Method notes in the README.
